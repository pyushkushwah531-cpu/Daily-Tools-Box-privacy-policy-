1. Introduction

Daily Tools Box is a utility application designed to provide helpful calculators and tools directly on your mobile device. This application operates entirely offline on your device.

2. 100% Offline Operation & Zero Data Collection

We respect your privacy completely. Daily Tools Box runs entirely offline.

  • No Internet Required: The app does not require an internet connection to function.
  • No Data Collection: We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information, device data, location, or usage analytics.
  • Local Processing: All calculations and tool operations happen locally in your device's memory. Your input data never leaves your device.

3. Advertisements & Third-Party Services

No Ads: Daily Tools Box contains no advertisements of any kind. We do not integrate any ad networks, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers.

4. Permissions

The app requires no special or dangerous permissions (such as Camera, Contacts, Location, or Storage access) to function. It operates safely within standard device sandboxing.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is safe for users of all ages. Because we collect zero data from anyone, there is no risk or collection of children's data.
